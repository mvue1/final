Welcome~! ;)

Written by Hadeon

Laura. You're quite the silly girl. It would've been easy if you just stuck to having no magic, but of course, you wanted to impress those around you. You wanted to have a form of protection. So what did you do? You went venturing the old ruins and came across the old library where I store my book. Oh, but of course, you didn't like the magic I had to offer. Of course!! You took the magic HE wrote. Typical. But you know, that still gives me a connection to your mind. I see everything you and your little friends are doing.

You're not slick with you ethics either. "I'm going to use this magic for good!" You're cute, but thats bullshit. You're using it for your own selfish means, just like I did. Playing with death isn't fun and games, dear. Even if you intend to use it for "good," the souls you're "borrowing" are still trapped. I guess the only good thing is that you're nice to them. If it were me, I wouldn't be so nice to things that are supposed to be long gone!

You know, you're not the only one in your little group I have connections to~. Ah, but I won't tell you who~! Where's the fun in spoiling information like that? He and I go wayyy back. Your face looks so funny right now. I love that look. You know, your father and I go way back too. Funny enough, it was me who burned down the village all those years ago. Although, little ol' you wouldn't remember something like that. You were just a sweet little baby. Thats how you met your parents now~. They put on a whole show for the jury; starring yours truly. I played the innocent role, but considering I still got trapped in an alternate dimension with the souls I had "borrowed" in the past, I guess we know what the jury and judge decided.

Don't get too used to your little boyfriend. Wasn't he blind before?? Strange how he seems to notice you more now, isn't it? And his brother? He seems awfully healthier now. I'm teasing you. But maybe you should consider my words.

Good luck, Laura. Have fun with death~
