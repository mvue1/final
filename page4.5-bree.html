Laura wanders the Land of Souls aimlessly. Tears stream down her face but she doesn't seem to understand why.

Taking over Hadeon's place in this land has caused her to become an empty, magical shell of herself.

A strange husk soul makes her feel things she doesn't want to feel, and she ultimately becomes ignorant and overly optimistic to avoid it.

Her body lacks any real empathy, but she tries to make this realm happier and brighter for the souls that are trapped with her.

All she can really do now, is wander with a smile and flowing tears.

When her dear friend Bree finds her, Laura doesn't recognize her.

Laura treats this new guest nicely, but Bree can tell theres something off about her.
