Laura wanders the Land of Souls aimlessly, fighting herself internally to regain control of her own body.

Taking over Hadeon's place in this land has caused her to become an empty, magical shell of herself.

One she wants to break free from.

Her body lacks any real empathy, but she tries to make this realm happier and brighter for the souls that are trapped with her.

All she can really do now, is wander with a smile.

When her dear friend Bree finds her, Laura bursts into tears, but eerily remains smiling and overly positive.
